DECLARATIONS AND STATEMENTS
============================

Manuscript: The Conditioning Set Decides the Measured Transfer Asymmetry: An Exact
Correction and a Source-Effect Reading for CNN versus Vision Transformer
Robustness Comparisons

Target: IEEE Access

Date: 2026-10-04 (revised)

---

1. DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

The experiments in this study use the CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets
[Krizhevsky, 2009] and the SVHN dataset [Netzer et al., 2011], all of which
are publicly available. Pre-trained WideResNet-28-10 weights were obtained
from the RobustBench benchmark [Croce et al., 2021].

---

2. CODE AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

The analysis pipeline, the scripts that generate every table and figure in
the manuscript, the per-sample evaluation records, the fixed
validation-split indices and the seed lists are publicly available at
https://github.com/cagrisahin58/cnn-vit-robustness-protocols
(MIT license for the code, CC BY 4.0 for the records). The per-sample
records include the transfer runs of the clean-trained model pairs, of the
eight-model RobustBench pool and of the last-checkpoint re-scoring, and the
repository also holds a machine-read copy of Tables 1 and 2 of Mahmood et
al. (ICCV 2021) with the scripts that decompose and re-score them. Together they
reproduce every number reported in the paper: all four conditioning
protocols are computed from the per-sample records, so the protocol
comparison can be repeated without retraining. Trained checkpoints total
about 40 GB and are available from the authors on reasonable request.
