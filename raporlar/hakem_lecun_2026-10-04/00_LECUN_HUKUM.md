## 1. Verdict

**Major revision.** Confidence: high (about 0.85 that a second round with the changes below ends in acceptance at IEEE Access; about 0.95 that the current build would be desk-returned or reviewed hostilely).

The sentence that decides it: the measurement layer is sound and largely reproducible, but the title, the abstract's main quantitative sentence and one of the two listed contributions are contradicted by the paper's own tables, and the reproducibility statement is false on the day of review; every fix is text, bookkeeping or a desk analysis, and none needs a new training run.

Why not Reject: the paper has a clean, falsifiable methodological point (the conditioning set must be named; the raw rate is a closed-form mixture of transferability and target clean error whose premise p can be measured, and here is 0.989 to 1.000), per-sample logs that let anyone recompute all four protocols, and three honest negative results. That is more than most CNN-versus-ViT papers deliver. Why not Minor: the abstract has to be rewritten around different facts, a listed contribution has to be withdrawn or inverted, and the PDF and the release are not in a submittable state.

## 2. What this paper actually shows

On one adversarially trained ResNet-18/ViT-Tiny pair, the choice of which samples enter the transfer denominator moves the measured CNN-to-ViT asymmetry from 4.4 to 19.4 points while reseeding moves it by at most 2.4, and nearly all of that spread is bookkeeping: 9.2 points are the target's clean error entering the raw rate through the law of total probability (premise p measured at 0.989 to 1.000 over 36 directions), the successful-source protocol estimates a different quantity, and what remains between the two defensible protocols is 3.9 points (run-level 95% t interval [3.1, 4.7], my recomputation from the per-seed values 3.68/3.77/4.27 in `results/c1_transfer/c1_transfer_summary.json`), itself a closed-form source-side composition term. The CNN-to-ViT direction on CIFAR is a source effect (ViT-crafted examples transfer worse to every target, including the external WRN), not a weaker-target effect, and the 15-point magnitude is a function of a 12-point clean gap produced by a ViT-Tiny trained from scratch and selected at epoch 18 of 100; on SVHN, where the gap is 1.85 points, the spread is 3.7 and the sign is noise. The genuinely transferable content is the reporting rule plus the correction, the measurement that an offline checkpoint-selection rule alone moves PGD-10 accuracy by 1.6 to 2.9 points on fixed weights, and three readings (spatial locality, attention degradation, TGR gain) that fail under direct measurement in this regime.

Would it survive ImageNet-scale models and modern recipes? The identity would, trivially, since it is arithmetic; the 15-point number would not, since it scales with a clean gap that well-trained pairs do not have. The paper should say exactly that, in the abstract.

The four-protocol design with per-sample logging and masks defined once is the right instrument and stays. Three seeds paired by index is an adequate and honest design; do not add cross-pairings or more runs for this paper. The behavioural sections keep their tables and their negative results; only the figures move. The absolute-robustness result (CNN advantage under every attack, McNemar in every run) stays as the stable anchor. Fig. 3 stays as the central figure. The WRN as the third model stays. The venue stays IEEE Access. No new GPU training is required for this revision, and none should be started before the desk analyses of action 6 are read.
