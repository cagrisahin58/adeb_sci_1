## Overall

**Lens D verdict: Major revision (presentation), not Reject.** The quantitative content of the tables is in good shape: every value I checked in Tables 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10 and 11 traces to a valid `c1_*`/`q1/*` artifact, internal arithmetic holds (Table 3 differences, Fig. 3 decomposition terms), and the paper's central figure (Fig. 3, protocol sets) is the best-made object in the manuscript.
