Manuscript: The Conditioning Set Decides the Measured Transfer Asymmetry: An Exact
Correction and a Matched-Pair Prediction for CNN versus Vision Transformer
Robustness Comparisons

Revised 2026-10-04. All figures come from results/C1_REFERANS_FOYU.md
(3 seeds, full test set, eps = 8/255 unless stated).

1. On fixed adversarially trained ResNet-18 and ViT-Tiny models, four
   conditioning protocols place the CNN-to-ViT transfer asymmetry between
   +4.4 and +19.4 points (mean per-pair spread 15.01 +/- 0.84 points),
   while retraining moves it across at most 2.41 points within any
   protocol (standard deviations 0.23 to 1.27 points).

2. The 9.21 points between unconditioned and target-correct estimates are
   predicted exactly by the law of total probability. Its premise p
   measures 0.989 to 1.000 on adversarially trained targets and falls to
   0.877 on standard-trained ones, where the p = 1 correction misses by up
   to 1.58 points.

3. The two endorsed protocols, target-correct and both-correct, still
   differ by 3.91 points (run-level 95% interval [3.12, 4.70]); the
   successful-source protocol estimates a different quantity and lies
   11.10 points above both-correct.

4. The direction is a source effect: additive fits give source terms of
   +5.13 (CIFAR-10) and +9.54 points (CIFAR-100) against target terms of
   -2.03 and -6.19; ViT-crafted examples also transfer worse to a fixed
   WideResNet-28-10 (8.65% against 12.48%).

5. The magnitude follows the clean-accuracy gap: the spread is 3.70 points
   on SVHN (1.85-point gap), 0.19 points for a standard-trained pair 0.47
   points apart, and over 28 pairs of an eight-model pool the
   three-protocol spread rises by 0.872 points per point of gap. The last
   checkpoint moves the spread from 15.01 to 12.84 points.

6. The CNN leads in absolute robustness under AutoAttack (37.93 +/- 0.14%
   against 29.14 +/- 0.40%), while three common readings fail under direct
   measurement: none of four spatial-locality measures differs at the run
   level, CLS attention entropy is equivalent in 12 of 12 layers, and a
   ViT-specific transfer attack gives no gain over MI-FGSM.
