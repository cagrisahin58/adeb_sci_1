DECLARATIONS AND STATEMENTS

Manuscript: The Measurement Protocol Decides the Conclusion: A Methodological
Study of CNN and Vision Transformer Adversarial Robustness Comparisons

Target: IEEE Access

Date: 2026-08-25 (revised)

---

1. DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

The experiments in this study use the CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets
[Krizhevsky, 2009] and the SVHN dataset [Netzer et al., 2011], all of which
are publicly available. Pre-trained WideResNet-28-10 weights were obtained
from the RobustBench benchmark [Croce et al., 2021].

---

2. CODE AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

Upon acceptance we will publish the analysis pipeline, the scripts that
generate every table and figure in the manuscript, the per-sample evaluation
records, the fixed validation-split indices and the seed lists. These
together reproduce every number reported in the paper: all four conditioning
protocols are computed from the per-sample records, so the protocol
comparison can be repeated without retraining. Trained checkpoints total
about 36 GB and are available from the authors on reasonable request.
