1. DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

The study uses the public CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 [Krizhevsky, 2009], SVHN
[Netzer et al., 2011] and ImageNetV2 MatchedFrequency [Recht et al., 2019]
datasets. The panel checkpoints for CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 and ImageNet come
from the RobustBench benchmark [Croce et al., 2021]. The ImageNet
initializations of the two-by-two design are the public DeiT-Tiny
[Touvron et al., 2021] and ResNet-18 A1 [Wightman et al., 2021] weights
distributed with the timm library.

---

2. CODE AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

The analysis pipeline, the scripts behind every table and figure, the
per-sample evaluation records, the validation-split indices and the seed
lists will be released in the public repository at
https://github.com/cagrisahin58/cnn-vit-robustness-protocols
(MIT license for the code, CC BY 4.0 for the records) upon acceptance.
The release will also hold the pre-registration documents with their
freeze records, the frozen analysis script, the cell tables whose SHA-256
digests the analysis outputs record, and a machine-read copy of Tables 1
and 2 of Mahmood et al. (ICCV 2021) with the scripts that re-score them.
Until then, the registration documents, the frozen analysis script and
the cell tables are time-stamped in the development repository (commits
e32f2ce and 7325419), and all of the above can be shared with the editor
and reviewers on request. Since every protocol and decomposition term is
computed from these records, all reported numbers can be reproduced
without retraining. Trained checkpoints are available from the authors on
request.

---

3. PRE-REGISTRATION

Predictions, tests and decision rules for the panel tests, the
architecture-by-initialization design, the attack-side test and the
masking check were frozen on 29 October 2026 before any of the
corresponding runs; an addendum covering CIFAR-100, ImageNetV2 and
gradient alignment was frozen on 30 October 2026 before any of its
matrices or gradients was computed. The registered tests ran through the
frozen analysis script or, for the addendum, through a wrapper around it
and a gradient script whose digests were recorded before their first run;
every analysis output stores the digests of the scripts that produced it.
Both registration documents and their SHA-256 freeze records will be
released in the public repository upon acceptance; until then they are
time-stamped in the development repository (commits e32f2ce and
7325419). The manuscript (Section III,
"Pre-Registration") lists the data seen before registration, the two
rules changed after data had been seen, and the one registered
sensitivity analysis computed after the results had been seen; analyses
outside the registration are labelled exploratory.

7. ETHICAL APPROVAL

Not applicable. This study uses only publicly available benchmark datasets
(CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, SVHN, ImageNetV2) and publicly released model
checkpoints, and does not involve human subjects, animals, or sensitive
data. The research measures adversarial vulnerability with the goal of
improving how the robustness of deep learning models is evaluated.

8. USE OF GENERATIVE AI TOOLS

As stated in the Acknowledgment of the manuscript, the authors used Claude
(Anthropic) during the preparation of this work. The tool assisted with
drafting and revising the manuscript text in all sections and with
implementing the analysis and verification scripts released with the
paper. The study design, the experimental protocol and all conclusions
are the authors', who verified every reported number against the released
artifacts. No figure was produced by an image-generation model.
