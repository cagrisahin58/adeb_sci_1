\begin{abstract}
CNN-versus-ViT transfer studies state which samples they score but rarely vary this conditioning set, leaving its weight unknown. We measure it on adversarially trained ResNet-18 and ViT-Tiny (three runs each, full CIFAR-10 test set, per-sample logging, validation split held out before training). With models, data and attack fixed, four conditioning protocols place the CNN-to-ViT transfer asymmetry between 4.4 and 19.4 points, whereas retraining moves it by at most 2.4 points (run-to-run SD 0.23 to 1.27). Three separate facts describe this range. The law of total probability predicts the 9.21 points between the unconditioned and target-correct estimates exactly; its premise $p$, that a clean-misclassified sample stays misclassified, measures 0.989 to 1.000 on adversarially trained targets but falls to 0.88 on standard-trained ones, where assuming $p=1$ errs by up to 1.6 points. The successful-source protocol estimates a different quantity, 11.10 points above both-correct. The two protocols we endorse differ by 3.91 points (run-level 95\% interval [3.12, 4.70]). The direction is a source effect: additive fits to target-correct matrices give source terms of $+5.13$ (CIFAR-10) and $+9.54$ points (CIFAR-100) against target terms of $-2.03$ and $-6.19$. The magnitude follows the clean-accuracy gap: the spread falls to 3.70 points for near-matched SVHN models and to 0.19 for a standard-trained pair 0.47 points apart, and over 28 pairs of an eight-model pool the identity term grows by 0.87 points per point of gap. Checkpoint selection moves the spread by about two points. Three further readings fail under direct measurement; we close with reporting requirements.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and Code Availability}
% 2026-10-02: depo HERKESE ACIK (cnn-vit-robustness-protocols; kurucu betik
% scripts/acik_depo_kur.sh). Kapsam vaatle ayni: analiz kodu + ornek bazinda
% kayitlar + bolme indeksleri + tohumlar; kontrol noktalari (40 GB) istek
% uzerine. Uc yerdeki dil ayni: simdiki zaman, ayni kapsam (burasi, §6, TR).
The experiments in this study use the CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets~\cite{krizhevsky2009learning} and the SVHN dataset~\cite{netzer2011reading}, all of which are publicly available. Pre-trained WideResNet-28-10 weights were obtained from the RobustBench benchmark~\cite{croce2021robustbench, gowal2020uncovering}. % 2026-10-04 (LeCun turu): depo kapsamina yeni artefaktlar eklendi (temiz ciftler,
% RobustBench havuzu, son kontrol noktasi, Mahmood tablolari ve betikleri).
The analysis pipeline, the scripts that generate every table and figure in this paper, the per-sample evaluation records, the fixed validation-split indices and the seed lists are publicly available at \texttt{https://github.com/cagrisahin58/cnn-vit-robustness-protocols}. The per-sample records include the transfer runs of the clean-trained model pairs, of the eight-model RobustBench pool and of the last-checkpoint re-scoring, and the repository also holds a machine-read copy of Tables~1 and~2 of Mahmood et al.~\cite{mahmood2021robustness} together with the scripts that decompose and re-score them. Together these reproduce every number reported here, and because the four conditioning protocols are all computed from the per-sample records, the protocol comparison can be repeated without retraining. Trained checkpoints total about $40$~GB and are available from the authors on request.
