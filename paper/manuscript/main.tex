\begin{abstract}
Adversarially trained ViTs look like weak transfer sources against CNNs, but two models cannot split this asymmetry between source and target. We factor log target-correct transfer rates into target vulnerability, symmetric similarity and directional residual, averaged over a fixed panel of seven robust ResNet-family CNNs, testing registered predictions with two or three runs per cell (brackets: 90\% intervals over training runs, conditional on test images and panel). On CIFAR-10, 0.936 [0.925, 0.948] of ViT-Tiny's panel source gap to ResNet-18 is symmetric, and the ViT's directional term ($+0.012$) is equivalent to zero within $\pm0.10$. In an architecture-by-initialization design on a matched pipeline, the scratch-trained ViT is less similar to the panel than the ResNet ($-0.364$ [$-0.415$, $-0.314$]), a contrast confounded with its lower clean accuracy. ImageNet initialization narrows it more for the ViT ($+0.260$); averaged over initialization, architecture leaves the log directional term within $\pm0.10$ ($+0.003$). The gap is retained under the best of PGD-10, momentum PGD and adapted token gradient regularization (0.960), and AutoAttack flags no new model for masking. On CIFAR-100 the symmetric share (0.840) replicates, but equivalence of the ViT's directional term is refuted ($+0.143$): there the ViT is also a directionally weaker source. A small directional term holds only on CIFAR-10, the log scale and a CNN panel; on the logit scale it is strongly negative. On ImageNetV2, transformers in two same-recipe pairs are descriptively less similar to the panel ($-0.142$), and input-gradient cosine tracks similarity on three datasets (Spearman 0.893--0.944 net of additive model effects).
\end{abstract}

% CIFAR-100 ve ImageNet modelleri, timm ImageNet baslatmalari eklendi; depo
% kapsamina on kayit dosyalari, dondurulmus cozumleme betigi ve hucre
% tablolari eklendi. "about 40 GB" foyde olmadigi icin sayisiz yazildi.
The CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100~\cite{krizhevsky2009learning}, SVHN~\cite{netzer2011reading} and ImageNetV2 MatchedFrequency~\cite{recht2019imagenet} datasets are public. Panel checkpoints come from RobustBench~\cite{croce2021robustbench}, and the ImageNet initializations are the public DeiT-Tiny~\cite{touvron2021training} and ResNet-18 A1~\cite{wightman2021resnet} weights from timm. The analysis pipeline, the scripts behind every table and figure, the per-sample evaluation records, the validation-split indices and the seed lists are public at \url{https://github.com/cagrisahin58/cnn-vit-robustness-protocols}. It also holds the pre-registration documents with their freeze records, the frozen analysis script, the cell tables whose SHA-256 digests the analysis outputs record, and a machine-read copy of Tables~1 and~2 of Mahmood et al.~\cite{mahmood2021robustness} with the scripts that re-score them. Since every protocol and decomposition term is computed from these records, all reported numbers can be reproduced without retraining. Trained checkpoints are available from the authors on request.
